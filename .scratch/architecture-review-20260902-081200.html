Candidate 1: Deepen the Spending Telemetry Engine

Why tackle first: Over 50% of recent commits focus on payday cycles, pace curves, and budget telemetry. Currently, DashboardViewModel is a 772-line class where complex financial domain calculations directly leak into UI state. Extracting a deep SpendingTelemetryEngine collapses the largest shallow module in the codebase, eliminates friction for ongoing chart features, and gives 100% pure in-memory test coverage over all financial pacing math without mocking UI or database adapters.
